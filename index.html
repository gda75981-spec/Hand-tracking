How to use

1. Tap Enable Camera & Start.

2. Allow camera access.

3. Hold one hand in front of your camera.

4. Move your index finger to move the star.

5. Pinch your thumb and index finger together.

Camera frames are processed locally by the hand-tracking library. This page does not intentionally record or upload video.
